# Blueprint Review and Approval Register

**Status:** Open. No discovery gate is marked approved by this document.

## Review sequence

| Review | Required evidence | Decision owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem and audience | Caregiver interviews and synthesis | Product owner | Open |
| Proposition and UX | Concept comparison and prototype findings | Product owner | Open |
| Age fit and safety | Content policy, benchmark, safety review | Product owner with qualified specialists | Open |

| Architecture | ADRs, data map, reliability and security design | Product owner | Open |
| Requirements baseline | Prioritised IDs and acceptance criteria | Product owner | Open |

| Delivery readiness | Estimates, owners, test strategy, operations | Product owner | Open |
| Launch readiness | Beta plan, stop conditions, support and marketing | Product owner | Open |

## Decision values

- **Approved:** evidence reviewed and scope accepted.
- **Rework:** changes or more evidence required.
- **Deferred:** intentionally postponed with a reason.
- **Rejected:** direction is not accepted.

## Approval record template

For each review, record:
- Review item and document version.
- Decision and date.
- Approver.
- Evidence reviewed.
- Accepted risks and open questions.
- Follow-up actions, owners, and target dates.

## Implementation lock

A general “please continue” authorises continuation of discovery work. It does not itself mean the product owner has approved the full blueprint or authorised application implementation. Implementation remains blocked until an explicit approval of the complete blueprint is recorded and the required gates are passed.
