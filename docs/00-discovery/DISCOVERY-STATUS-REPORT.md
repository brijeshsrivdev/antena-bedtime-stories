## Evidence not yet collected

These are plans, not completed activities:
- Adult caregiver interviews and concept testing.
- Audio samples generated under controlled conditions.
- Independent quality scoring and measured provider cost.
- Device tests for screen-off playback and recovery.
- Provider contract, data retention, rights, and regional review.
- Jurisdiction-specific legal review.
- Pricing validation with caregivers.
- A validated acquisition channel.

Do not present any of these as completed or use assumed results to finalise architecture.

## Recommended next work sequence

1. Recruit and interview adult caregivers using the interview guide.
2. Synthesize evidence and decide whether to narrow the target segment or proposition.
3. Create the common audio benchmark set and scorecard.
4. Run provider tests and calculate accepted-story cost, including failed attempts.
5. Test the core listening journey on candidate platforms.
6. Resolve launch jurisdiction and language with evidence.
7. Update the MVP scope, requirement baseline, ADRs, and cost model.
8. Review and approve each discovery gate.
9. Present the complete blueprint for explicit owner approval.
10. Only then begin implementation planning and coding.

## Blockers to approval

The largest blockers are product evidence, provider feasibility, launch market/platform, and commercial assumptions. More documentation alone cannot replace caregiver feedback or measured audio output.

## Owner decisions eventually required

- Primary caregiver segment and target age band.
- Core proposition and minimum story/audio quality bar.
- Initial platform and launch jurisdiction/language.
- Provider strategy and cost ceiling.
- Monetisation model and initial pricing.
- MVP scope and delivery budget.
- Acceptance of residual risks and explicit implementation approval.

## Rule

A request to continue authorises more discovery work. It does not, by itself, approve the blueprint or unlock application implementation.
