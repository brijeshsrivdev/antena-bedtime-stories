## What cannot honestly be completed from documents alone

- Interviews cannot be marked complete without participants.
- Audio quality cannot be ranked without actual comparable outputs.
- API cost cannot be validated from example pricing alone.
- Platform choice cannot be validated without device tests and user needs.
- Legal compliance cannot be signed off without a chosen jurisdiction and qualified review.
- Pricing cannot be approved from hypothetical interest alone.

## Current next action

Prepare recruitment and interview operations, then conduct adult caregiver research. In parallel, prepare the benchmark scripts and verify current provider access/terms. Do not start child-facing product implementation during this phase.

## Escalation rule

If recruitment, provider access, budget, or legal review is blocked, record the blocker and propose the smallest discovery experiment that can resolve it. Do not silently substitute assumptions for evidence.
