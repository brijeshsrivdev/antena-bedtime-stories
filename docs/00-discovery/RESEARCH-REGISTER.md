# Evidence and Research Register

## Research quality rules
- Record claim, source, source type, publication date, retrieval date, geography, and confidence.
- Prefer primary sources: official documentation, terms/licences, government/regulator guidance, company pricing pages, and original research.
- Treat competitor-authored comparisons as leads, not independent proof.
- Separate facts, estimates, opinions, and hypotheses.
- Verify changing capabilities and pricing at decision time.
- Record contradictory evidence and what would falsify a recommendation.

## Initial research leads (11 October 2026)

### AI/audio platform
- Google, “Build real-time voice applications with Gemini Audio,” published 15 September 2026: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/build-real-time-voice-applications-gemini-audio/ . Describes Gemini Live models, transcription, configurable TTS, music generation, and multilingual capabilities. **Implication:** evaluate Google’s offerings as candidates, not as an assumed single-provider architecture. Live speech-to-speech is not automatically the right engine for pre-authored, scene-controlled bedtime narration. Verify exact API availability, pricing, quotas, commercial terms, regional support, and output rights in official documentation.
- Google, “Improved Gemini audio models for powerful voice interactions”: https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-audio-model-updates/ . Useful background; current model details must be re-verified before decisions.

### Competitor discovery leads
- Storyboat, “Best Bedtime Story Apps for Kids in 2026,” published 1 October 2026: https://storyboat.app/blog/best-bedtime-story-apps . Competitor-authored comparison of Storyboat, Moshi, Vooks, Readmio, Epic, Yoto, Oscar Stories, and Bedtimestory.ai. Use to identify products for direct review; do not treat its rankings as independent.
- Bedtime Stories, “The Best Bedtime Story & Storytelling Apps for Kids (2026)”: https://www.bedtime-stories.fun/blog/best-bedtime-story-apps . Competitor content; use as a discovery lead only.
- Gula Stories, “Best Bedtime Story Apps in 2026”: https://www.gulastories.com/blog/best-bedtime-story-apps-2026/ . Includes dated price observations, but all prices must be checked against each provider’s official store/page and relevant country.

## Tracks still requiring primary research
- Customer discovery with parents/caregivers; avoid collecting identifying details from children during early research.
- Official product pages and app-store listings for each competitor, with region-specific pricing and feature verification.
- Official AI provider documentation, API pricing, terms, safety policies, rate limits, and commercial-use rights.
- Official child privacy, consumer subscription, accessibility, and app-store requirements for each candidate launch market; obtain qualified legal review.
- Cost model based on measured tokens, generated audio duration, retries, storage, delivery, and replay behaviour.

## Research record template
For each finding record: ID; question; claim; source URL; publisher/author; publication and retrieval dates; region/population; method and limitations; confidence; product implication; counter-evidence; and next validation step.
