# Caregiver Recruitment and Consent Materials

**Status:** Draft for adult research participants. Adapt and review before use.

## Short recruitment message

We are researching how families experience bedtime stories and audio routines. We are speaking with adult parents and caregivers for a voluntary 25–30 minute conversation. We want to understand current routines, what works, what is frustrating, and what would make an audio story product trustworthy.

This is research, not a sales pitch. The product is still in discovery and may change or may never launch. Participation is optional. We do not need your child's name, photo, voice, school, or other identifying information. Please do not share those details.

If you are interested, reply with a convenient time and your preferred language.

## Opening script

Thank you for considering this conversation. We are exploring bedtime routines and story experiences. There are no right or wrong answers, and critical feedback is useful. The concept is not a finished product.

Please avoid sharing names or identifying details about your child or family. You can skip any question or stop at any time.

With your permission, I will take anonymised notes for product research. The notes will be used to identify patterns, not to profile you. We will not publish your name or identify you in reports. Do you agree to continue and to have anonymised notes taken?

Record the participant's answer separately from research notes. If recording is proposed, obtain separate explicit consent, explain storage and deletion, and prefer not to record for this early study.

## Data minimisation

Collect only:
- Participant code.
- Broad caregiver context needed for the research.
- Preferred language and availability.
- Answers relevant to the research questions.
- Consent status and date, stored separately.

Do not collect:
- Child names or exact dates of birth.
- Child photos, voice recordings, school or location.
- Health information or sensitive family details.
- Contact lists or unrelated demographic information.

## Participant rights and retention

Explain the intended retention period before research begins. Keep contact details separate from interview notes. Restrict access to the research owner. Delete contact details when follow-up is complete, unless the participant separately opts in to future contact. Honour withdrawal requests where practicable and explain any limits before consent.

## Closing script

Thank you. Your feedback will help us decide whether this problem is worth solving and what to test next. We cannot promise the product will launch. Would you like to receive a brief summary of findings or be contacted about a future adult caregiver test? This is optional and separate from your participation.
