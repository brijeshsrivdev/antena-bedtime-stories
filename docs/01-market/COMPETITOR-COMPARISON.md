## Early observations

- Personalised AI stories are already a visible category; “AI-generated stories” alone is unlikely to be defensible.
- Public pricing for direct AI story competitors spans subscription and usage/coin approaches. These are price anchors, not proof of willingness to pay for our audio quality proposition.
- The key differentiation to validate is scene-level audio production and calming playback, not simply story generation.
- Screen-free alternatives matter because parents may prefer a physical audio device or existing audio subscription.

## Gaps before a decision

- Directly test current apps on iOS/Android/web and record the date, region, and plan.
- Verify full audio experience, latency, story coherence, voice consistency, controls, offline mode, age filters, privacy policy, and cancellation path.
- Compare parent reviews, complaints, and store ratings with sample size and dates.
- Verify app-store and website pricing separately.
- Conduct interviews before deciding the target segment, launch market, or price.
