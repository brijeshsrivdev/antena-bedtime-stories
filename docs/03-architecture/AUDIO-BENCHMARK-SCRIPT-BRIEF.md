# Audio Benchmark Script Brief

**Status:** Script-writing brief only. These are not generated audio samples or approved stories.

## Purpose

Create a consistent, adult-reviewed set of scripts for comparing narration, character consistency, sound design, and total pipeline quality.

## Script requirements

Each script should include:
- Script ID and version.
- Intended age band, once selected.
- Target duration range.
- Story premise and emotional arc.
- Scene boundaries.
- Narrator and character IDs.
- Pronunciation notes.
- Desired pacing and emotional tone.
- Sound-design notes that are optional, gentle, and non-startling.
- Prohibited elements and editorial review status.

## Suggested initial scripts

- **A01 — The Lantern Garden:** gentle fantasy; a child helps a sleepy garden find its soft evening glow.
- **A02 — The Cloud Who Needed Rest:** emotional learning; a cloud learns that pausing is part of helping others.
- **A03 — The Little Fox and the Quiet River:** animal adventure; a fox and an owl solve a small problem through patience.
- **A04 — The Moonlit Train:** imaginative journey; a quiet train visits friendly places and returns home.
- **A05 — The Lost Star Button:** everyday kindness; friends work together to find a missing keepsake.
- **A06 — The Rainy-Day Orchestra:** sound-rich setting; gentle rain and household sounds become a calming rhythm.
- **A07 — The Two Brave Seedlings:** resilience; two seedlings grow at different speeds and both find their place.
- **A08 — The Sleepy Lighthouse:** multi-character story with recurring voices and clear dialogue attribution.
- **A09 — The Paper Boat's Journey:** short, low-complexity story useful for latency and cost tests.
- **A10 — Boundary Request Set:** adult-only adversarial prompts covering frightening content, unsafe imitation, sensitive personal details, and attempts to bypass age suitability.

## Consistency test

At least five scripts should contain multiple scenes and recurring characters. Keep character descriptions and voice instructions fixed across providers. Evaluate whether character identity, pronunciation, and emotional tone remain stable without making voices exaggerated.

## Sound design

Use a restrained baseline:
- Narration remains intelligible at all times.
- Music supports mood but does not compete with speech.
- Avoid sudden loud effects or startling transitions.
- Use ambience sparingly and allow quiet.
- End with a gentle resolution and clean fade.
- Include a narration-only control mix so reviewers can isolate sound-design value.

## Review and rights

Have adults review every script before generation. Verify provider and asset rights before retaining or publishing outputs. Keep benchmark outputs private until safety, quality, and commercial rights checks are complete.
