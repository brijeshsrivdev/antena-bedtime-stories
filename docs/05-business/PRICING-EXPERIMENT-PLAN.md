# Caregiver Pricing Experiment Plan

**Status:** Research plan. No price or monetisation model approved.

## Questions to answer

- Will caregivers pay for professionally produced bedtime audio?
- Is the value mainly personalised generation, replay, screen-off convenience, or trust?
- Do families prefer a free allowance, a trial, subscription, or one-time purchase?
- What monthly usage do families consider reasonable?
- Does a visible generation allowance create confidence or feel restrictive?
- What cancellation and refund expectations must be supported?

## Research sequence

1. Ask about past purchases and current spending before presenting prices.
2. Explain the proposed product consistently and disclose that it is a concept.
3. Test a small set of price points selected for the chosen launch market.
4. Ask what feels too cheap to trust, reasonable, expensive, and unacceptable.
5. Ask participants to choose between a clearly described trial, free allowance, and subscription.
6. Follow stated intent with a real landing-page or waitlist test before claiming demand.
7. If possible, test a refundable deposit or paid pilot only after legal and operational review.

## Price-test safeguards

- Do not misrepresent product availability.
- Do not charge for a product that is not ready.
- Do not use deceptive countdowns, hidden renewals, or confusing cancellation.
- Disclose story-generation limits and replay terms clearly.
- Keep billing decisions in parent-controlled flows.
- Separate curiosity from willingness to pay and actual conversion.

## Economic guardrails

Set these before choosing price:
- Maximum cost per accepted story.
- Expected stories generated per paying family.
- Retry and rejection allowance.
- Storage and audio delivery cost.
- Payment/store fees, taxes, refunds, and chargebacks.
- Support and safety-review cost.
- Minimum contribution margin.
- A stop condition if high-usage families make the plan unsustainable.

## Initial commercial hypothesis

A simple family subscription with a transparent monthly generation allowance and replay of saved stories may be easier to explain than opaque credits. This is only a hypothesis. A free starter library may help families evaluate audio quality before paying.

## Output

Produce a pricing memo with segment, tested prices, sample limitations, observed behaviour, conversion evidence, cost scenarios, proposed offer, and unresolved risks. Product owner approval is required.
