# Provisional Roadmap

**Status:** Planning sequence only. Dates and effort estimates are intentionally deferred until discovery and validation.

## Phase 0 — Discovery and evidence (current)
- Product charter, audience hypotheses, research plan, competitor analysis.
- Parent/caregiver interviews and concept tests.
- Audio quality rubric and provider benchmark plan.
- UI/UX journeys and prototype validation plan.
- Privacy, child safety, app-store, licensing, and launch-market research.
- **Exit:** Gates A and B approved.

## Phase 1 — Feasibility validation (no production implementation yet)
- Evaluate story generation, TTS, music/effects sources, voice consistency, latency, cost, rights, and fallback options.
- Conduct controlled audio listening tests with adults/parents; use appropriate consent and safeguards for any child research.
- Validate end-to-end cost model and playback architecture with documented evidence.
- **Exit:** Gate C approved.

## Phase 2 — Product and delivery specification
- Finalise MVP boundaries and prioritised requirements.
- Complete UX design, architecture decisions, API/data contracts, threat model, provider evaluation, test strategy, operational model, and traceability.
- Define multi-agent/session ownership, PR gates, and quality criteria.
- **Exit:** Gate D approved; owner explicitly authorises implementation.

## Phase 3 — MVP implementation (not authorised yet)
- Deliver only approved end-to-end vertical slices.
- Validate safe story planning, audio quality, reliable playback, favourites, parent controls, reporting, and cost telemetry.
- No scope expansion without decision-log approval.

## Phase 4 — Private beta
- Recruit a small, consented group of families through parent/caregiver channels.
- Track audio failures, content reports, satisfaction, completion, voluntary replay, and cost-to-serve.
- Run incident response and deletion exercises.
- **Exit:** safety, reliability, unit economics, and parent trust meet pre-agreed release thresholds.

## Phase 5 — Launch readiness
- Legal/privacy review for launch jurisdictions.
- App-store review assets, parental information, support workflows, billing/refunds, privacy policy, terms, accessibility, analytics, and operational runbooks.
- Confirm rights for all narration, music, effects, and generated assets.
- **Exit:** Gate E approved.

## Phase 6 — Launch and learning
- Controlled launch in one validated geography and a small set of proven languages/age bands.
- Measure acquisition quality, activation, completed listening, replay, parent satisfaction, retention, refunds, safety reports, and contribution margin.
- Expand markets, languages, age bands, or interactive features only when quality and compliance are proven.

## Roadmap principles
- Do not promise dates before provider tests, scope, and capacity are known.
- Validate one initial segment before trying to serve every age and language equally.
- Keep child safety, privacy, and audio quality as release gates, not post-launch fixes.
