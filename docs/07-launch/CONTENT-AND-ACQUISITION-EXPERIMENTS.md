# Low-Budget Content and Acquisition Experiments

**Status:** Proposed experiments. No channel or spend is approved.

## Principle

Earn caregiver trust with the actual listening experience. Avoid broad paid campaigns until the product has evidence of value, a reliable demo, and clear unit economics.

## Experiment 1 — Landing page and sample audio

- Publish a clear caregiver-focused promise and one reviewed sample.
- Explain AI use, privacy principles, and whether the sample is generated or curated.
- Offer an optional adult waitlist signup.
- Measure qualified visits, sample starts/completions, signup conversion, and questions.
- Do not use child tracking or misleading claims.

## Experiment 2 — Caregiver interviews and concept test

- Recruit through adult communities, personal referrals, and relevant groups with moderator permission.
- Share a short neutral concept card and audio sample.
- Ask what feels useful, concerning, or unclear.
- Record objections and reasons not to use it.
- Avoid unsolicited mass posting or asking children to submit personal information.

## Experiment 3 — Audio craft content

Create short demonstrations that explain one design choice at a time:
- How narration, ambience, and pacing work together.
- How recurring character voices are kept consistent.
- Why stories are validated before playback.
- How a screen-off bedtime flow works.
- What data is and is not required.

Do not publish provider output until quality and commercial rights are checked.

## Experiment 4 — Search content

Test a small number of genuinely useful caregiver articles, such as:
- How to build a screen-free bedtime routine.
- Choosing age-appropriate bedtime stories.
- What parents should know about AI-generated stories.
- How to use audio without encouraging extra screen time.

Avoid medical promises and keyword-stuffed mass content.

## Experiment 5 — Trusted partnerships

Explore a small number of conversations with storytellers, librarians, educators, family-focused creators, or parent groups. Be transparent about the product stage and do not imply endorsement without permission.

## Prioritisation

Score each experiment by:
- Cost to run.
- Time to learn.
- Fit with the intended caregiver segment.
- Ability to measure qualified interest.
- Trust and compliance risk.
- Follow-up workload.

Run one or two experiments at a time. Do not split a small budget across every channel.

## Metrics

- Qualified caregiver landing visits.
- Sample listen-through.
- Waitlist conversion.
- Interview completion.
- Strong positive and negative reasons.
- Beta opt-in and activation.
- Trial-to-paid conversion only after the product and billing are ready.
- Acquisition cost and contribution margin after launch.

Follower count, impressions, and raw downloads are not proof of product value.
