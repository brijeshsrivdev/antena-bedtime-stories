# Go-to-Market and Marketing Strategy — Initial Hypotheses

**Status:** Directional planning; launch market and budget are not selected.

## Positioning hypothesis
“Your child’s own magical bedtime storyteller—with rich, calming audio and stories they can revisit.” Validate this against parent language before adopting it as final copy.

## Likely buyer and discovery channels to test
- Parents/caregivers searching for bedtime stories, screen-free routines, kids audio, and personalised stories.
- Parenting newsletters, family creators, librarians/storytellers, and parent communities.
- Short audio demonstrations showing the difference between flat narration and a carefully mixed story.
- Search-focused educational content for parents, with transparent AI and privacy explanations.
- App-store optimisation with clear age range, sample audio, parent controls, subscription terms, and privacy details.

## Launch sequence
1. Interview parents before spending on ads.
2. Test a landing page with 2–3 positioning variants and a sample audio experience.
3. Build a waitlist only if it provides a useful measure of demand.
4. Run a small private beta and gather parent feedback with consent.
5. Publish a limited launch with a defined age band, language set, and geography.
6. Scale only channels with sustainable acquisition cost and positive retention/satisfaction signals.

## Content strategy
- Publish original short audio samples and behind-the-scenes sound design examples.
- Create parent-focused content on choosing age-appropriate stories and building calm routines, avoiding medical claims.
- Use adult-facing marketing surfaces; do not design marketing messages to pressure children into asking parents to pay.
- Obtain explicit rights/releases for voices, music, illustrations, and creator partnerships.

## Metrics
- Landing-page visit-to-waitlist conversion.
- Cost per qualified parent signup and beta activation.
- First story generated and first story completed.
- Parent-reported satisfaction and trust.
- Voluntary replay/favourite rate and return use.
- Paid conversion, refunds, cancellation reasons, and contribution margin.
- Safety reports, support response time, and audio failure rate.

## Decisions still open
- Brand/product name and trademark/domain availability.
- Initial launch geography and language.
- Mobile distribution strategy and app-store category.
- Free allowance, trial, subscription, and pricing.
- Marketing budget and channel experiments.
- Customer support, moderation, and incident response ownership.
